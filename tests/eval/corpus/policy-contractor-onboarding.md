# Contractor Onboarding & Engagement Policy

This policy governs how DFFRNT engages independent contractors for client work.

## Screening

Every contractor must sign the standard NDA and complete reliability screening within 10 business days of contract signature. Contractors must not access client materials before screening completes.

## Rate bands

Approved hourly rate bands:

- Junior: up to $60/hour
- Intermediate: $60–$90/hour
- Senior: $90–$120/hour

Rates above $120/hour require written approval from the managing partner.

## Invoicing and duration

Invoices are payable net-30. Timesheets are due every Friday in Harvest. A continuous engagement may not exceed 12 months without a conversion review by HR.
